mkonnect is now deployed.

Required configuration:
  - image.repository: your published registry (e.g. public.ecr.aws/<alias>/memento-connector)
  - config.gatewayUrl: wss://<your-gateway>/ws
  - config.connectorId: UUID of this connector

Register this connector via the Memento portal to receive a REGISTRATION_TOKEN,
then upgrade: helm upgrade ... --set config.registrationToken=<token>

After first connection, the connector stores its ML-DSA-65 key in the PVC at {{ .Values.config.keyFile }}.
